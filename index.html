How the Custom Process Works

  • 1

    Share Your Concept

    Submit your desired garment canvas, reference photos, color tones, and description of your dream motif.

  • 2

    Consultation & Sketching

    Bindu reviews your concept and reaches out with rough sketches and fabric options. You can schedule a direct call to align ideas.

  • 3

    Painting & Curing

    Once approved, the garment is hand-stretched and painted. Curing, steam-pressing, and finishing takes roughly **2 weeks**.

About Kalaa by Bindu

The Artist's Journey

Meet Bindu

Hello, I'm Bindu. Welcome to my studio. Fabric painting is a passion that I followed which later became a therapeutic journey for me. What started as a simple creative hobby soon transformed into a space of complete warmth, focus, and meditation.

Fabric painting is my space of warmth and calm. Using specialized brushes and custom-blended fabric paints, I ensure that the artwork is permanently bound to the threads while leaving the fabric as soft and breathable as it was on the loom.

Creating wearable art is a slow, thoughtful craft. By translating hand-drawn illustrations onto premium silks, cottons, and canvas, I hope to bring a piece of that organic calm and artistic beauty directly into your daily life.
